Our Kitchen

Where smoke, spice and a hot grill do all the talking.

At New Grill In the grill is the heart of the kitchen. Marinades go on early, the charcoal gets hot, and everything hits the flame to order — so the char is real and the flavor lands deep.

From smoky grilled plates to loaded burgers, bubbling pizza and stacked sandwiches, it's honest, generous food made to be eaten hot and shared loud.
